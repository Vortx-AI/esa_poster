This work investigates how agentic AI can build a 
connected EO ecosystem, using EVE (Earth Virtual 
Expert), a domain-specialized LLM funded by ESA's 
Φ-lab, to autonomously interact with EO tools, APIs, and 
databases via the Model Context Protocol, enabling 
dynamic reasoning and cross-source information retrieval.
