16:05–16:45 Panel: Earth Observation in the age of Agentic AI: From Insight to Business

From data to insight, and from insight to action: The panel will explore how Agentic AI could fundamentally reshape the EO value chain, from sensing and data generation to analysis, decision-making and business impact. Bringing together perspectives from research, EO technology and enterprise, the discussion will examine how AI agents could be a key element of the full data chain, combine EO with diverse sources of knowledge, and transform increasingly rich Earth observations into decision-ready intelligence. With applications ranging from insurance and risk management to emerging EO services and markets, the panel will address the key question: How do we move from technological potential to real-world impact and sustainable business value?

Moderated by Chiara Cocchiara, ESA Φ-lab

Panelists:

  • Julia Gottfriedsen Chief Technical Officer at ConstelIR
  • Marcin Detyniecki Group Chief Data Scientist & Head of Research & Advanced AI at AXA
  • Andres Camero Co-Head of Department EO Data Science at German Aerospace Center (DLR)
  • Linda Sheard VP, Analytics and Agentic Products at Xoople
17:00–17:30 Thematic Discussion: Subway Takes: Agentic AI Edition

Join this guided, engaging discussion where participants share and debate strong opinions, concerns, and unpopular takes on Agentic AI for Earth Observation. Come with a take of your own, or respond to one raised by someone else. The goal is not consensus, but a better collective understanding of where agentic approaches genuinely add value in EO.

Moderated by Eva Gmelich Meijling and Riccardo D’Ercole, ESA Φ-lab
