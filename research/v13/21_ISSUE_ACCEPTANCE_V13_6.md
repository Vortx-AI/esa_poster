## Verification and retained limits

`poster/build_v13_report.json` is the authoritative print report. `evidence/v136/site_review.json` records eight pages at 390 and 1440 pixels, exact clipboard equality, timed demo results, no external demo requests, and accessibility fallbacks. `poster/tests/` contains 14 release-gate tests, including deliberate overclaims and stale/unsupported integration copy. The CI workflow runs the evidence gate and these tests. The PDF was inspected through a Poppler render, including the new boundary and community band; the phone demo was visually inspected.

The new control counts are recomputed from the committed R5 result, not new model runs. Earlier Python/Rust results, receipt checks, community listing checks and failed ChatGPT connector attempts keep their original dates and scopes.

This release does not close #7 (binding upstream artifact hashes), #43 (separately operated agent-host demonstration), #39 (the complete media pack), or #46 (platform marks). It also does not treat all requested named experimental integrations as working deployments: Cline and Semantic Kernel retain qualified directory entries, so #40 remains open. #44 still calls for a complete external mobile install-path audit; local layout and historical HTTP responses are not substituted for that audit. Other benchmark, taxonomy and walk-by criteria remain open unless separately demonstrated.

The PR closes the accepted issues only after merging the verified files. Pages publication and the downloadable PDF are checked against the committed bytes. Artifact hashes and numerical validation totals are recorded in `poster/release_v13.6.json`.
