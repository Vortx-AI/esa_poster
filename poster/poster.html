Hand over the address, not the sentence.

emem is Earth memory for AI agents. Every answer about a place — a pixel value, a field, a field through time — comes back as a name derived from its own signed bytes. Agents pass the name. Any receiver re-hashes and verifies it offline, without trusting the sender, its model, or the server.

1 · The invariant

The bytes are their own name.

{ kind: "primary", cell: "defi.zb493.xuqA.zcb5f", // Bengaluru band: "copdem30m.elevation_mean", tslot: 0, // valid time value: 918.0, unit: "m", sources: [{ scheme: "open_meteo" }], derivation: { fn_key: "open_meteo_copdem90m@1" }, signer: ed25519:777er3yi…, signed_at: "2026-05-28T19:54:32Z" // txn time }
encodeemem-CBOR, 509 bytes hashBLAKE3-256 → base32 nameyqbolgeoycqkvj3zkxukb4bjw4odhpwvfzqo3fbgwf4spk45zala
Same bytes → same name. Stock BLAKE3, no emem code, no registry.
918.0 → 918.1 gives a different name (vines36l…).
Cited under the wrong cell → HTTP 409. Never a silent wrong answer.

Deterministic CBOR in declaration order, NaN/−0 canonical (not RFC 8949 key-sorted). signer and signed_at are hashed, so a CID names one signed attestation; the ed25519 signature covers a Merkle root of fact CIDs.

2 · The receiver

Resolve. Don’t trust.

  1. Resolve the token → the exact bytes
    GET /v1/facts/<cid>
  2. Re-hash them yourself. Does the name match?
  3. Bind the place: the token’s cell must equal the cell inside the signed fact.
  4. Verify the receipt offline: ed25519 over a domain-separated, length-prefixed preimage.
  5. Check history: RFC 6962-style log, 2.54 M entries, co-signed by an independent operator (geo.qa).
Crosses
84 characters — or one emem:bundle: handle, 38 chars for up to 256 facts.
Does not need to
the sender’s prompt, context, model, database, or honesty.

A proof-less receipt signs an explicit ABSENT marker, so stripping a proof is detectable. An independent audit rejected 5/5 tampers, including a version downgrade.

4 · The finding

A paraphrase made two models agree — and both chose wrong.

full context (control)72/72 · 36/36
compaction, free reasoning20/72 · 15/36
compaction under pressure0/72 · 3/36, p = 0.035

Agreement between agents is not evidence of correctness. It is what a shared lossy summary produces.

One real Lahaul NDVI fact; Gemma-4-12B and Qwen2.5-7B on one host; Fisher one-sided; six scorer bugs fixed before the verdict.

6 · Boundary of the claim

What holds. What doesn’t.

Checkable

  • byte integrity of a cited fact or field
  • where and when it claims to be
  • who signed it, and when
  • history not rewritten
  • “nothing here” — signed absence
  • disagreement kept and scored

Not proved

  • that the observation is true
  • sensor or model accuracy
  • two sensors saw one thing
  • a sentence cites the right band
  • anything in orbit (edge signing is simulated: 0.17% of a 40 ms frame)

Embeddings (live: TESSERA 128-D; earlier Clay v1.5, Prithvi-EO-2.0, Galileo) are signed as model_output facts — a learned representation, not a measurement. Open: formal proofs, full conformance vectors, hardware attestation.
